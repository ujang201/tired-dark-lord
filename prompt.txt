Write a 100-word monologue spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. The tiredness must live in the voice, rhythm, pauses and small deflections — never a plain statement that he is weary. He is a specific villain with a history and a scheme: thirty years keeping the Ember Crown burning, a rebellion he forgot to crush, generals waiting in the hall with swords he sharpened when the hero's grandfather was a boy. Concrete fantasy detail, no stock scenery. Speakable dialogue a performer could deliver in one breath, with a turn or sting in the last line. Exactly about 100 words, every word earning its place.
